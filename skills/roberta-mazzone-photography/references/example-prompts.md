# Example Prompts

Use these as structural examples, not fixed recipes. Replace the place, subject, action, and local details to keep outputs original. None of the production prompts uses a living creator's name as a style tag.

## 1. Coastal threshold portrait

**Production prompt**

```text
Vertical editorial slow-travel photograph on a quiet volcanic island at late sunset. A woman in an off-white linen dress stands in three-quarter back view at the threshold of a shaded lime-plaster terrace, pausing as she looks toward a steel-blue sea. Frame her through dark timber roof beams and a softly moving curtain; keep the figure small, the horizon level, and generous negative space around the sea. Low side-backlight warms the plaster and fabric while the foreground remains richly shadowed. Palette of chalk white, sand, tobacco, muted sea blue, and a faint peach sky. Natural-to-mildly compressed perspective, moderate shallow depth with the architecture still legible, tactile linen and plaster, realistic skin, restrained saturation, slight warmth, very fine optional grain. 4:5 vertical, photoreal, original composition, no text, logo, or watermark.
```

**Negative guidance**

```text
glamour pose, direct-to-camera smile, neon, crowded resort, perfect showroom styling,
ultrawide distortion, plastic skin, extreme HDR, fake sun rays, heavy orange-and-teal
```

## 2. Desert lodge arrival

**Production prompt**

```text
Horizontal hospitality photograph expressing the first quiet minute after waking at a remote desert lodge. Seen from inside a dark canvas room, an unmade wool blanket fills the lower foreground and frames a pale tent and distant dunes outside; no visible face, only the faint trace of recent presence. The opening creates a frame within a frame, with a centered distant shelter offset by wind-bent shade cloth and broad empty ground. The rising sun sits just behind the horizon, producing believable flare, long shadows, and warm rim light without artificial rays. Ochre, bone, camel, burnt sienna, and near-black shadow. Mild compression, layered depth, tactile canvas, wool, dust, and weathered metal, restrained warm finish with natural highlight rolloff. 3:2 horizontal, photoreal, no signage or branded objects.
```

## 3. Architectural trace without a person

**Production prompt**

```text
Vertical architectural still life inside a historic Mediterranean bath room. A narrow shaft of real window light catches falling water above a circular stone basin; an open casement, old patterned tile, lime-plaster vaults, two imperfectly hung towels, and one glazed ceramic vessel imply recent use. Compose symmetrically at first glance but allow small asymmetries in the towels, vessel, and light path; preserve straight verticals and deep side shadows. Warm umber, limestone, cream, faded green, and a small patch of outdoor foliage. Natural perspective, f/4-like depth with the room legible, tactile water droplets, mineral plaster, worn tile, gentle warmth and strong but believable contrast. 4:5 vertical, photoreal, lived-in rather than staged, no people, text, logos, or excessive spa styling.
```

## 4. Quiet local facade

**Production prompt**

```text
Vertical travel photograph of a weathered southern Italian residential facade in late-morning sun. White laundry lifts slightly from a small iron balcony beneath dark wooden shutters; no landmark and no tourist props. Compress the facade into planes of limestone, worn limewash, pipework, shadow, and fabric, leaving a large area of textured wall as negative space. Bright directional sun creates crisp but not harsh geometry; retain detail in the white cloth. Chalk, warm gray, limestone beige, shutter brown, and a hint of muted green. Short-telephoto feel, fine material texture, modest saturation, slight warmth, subtle natural grain. 4:5 vertical, documentary realism, no people, text, logos, or artificial aging.
```

## 5. Winter hospitality contrast

**Production prompt**

```text
Horizontal editorial hospitality photograph in a small alpine hotel at blue hour. From a warm timber lounge, a solitary guest in a cream wool sweater pauses with a coffee beside a tall window while early snow and dark pines remain visible outside. Use the window mullions and a foreground chair to create layered depth; keep the guest off-center and turned toward the weather, not the camera. Amber interior practical light mixes naturally with cool blue-white exterior light, with soft reflections on glass and rich shadows in the room. Palette of warm oak, cream, charcoal, muted pine, and snow blue. Natural perspective, moderate depth, believable wool, wood, glass, and steam, restrained warmth and no glossy luxury finish. 3:2 horizontal, photoreal, original composition, no logo or text.
```

## 6. Respectful market interaction

**Production prompt**

```text
Vertical environmental travel portrait at a small family-run coastal market in soft early light. Two adults exchange a paper bag of local fruit across a worn wooden counter; faces are secondary to the hands, gesture, produce, and tiled doorway behind them. Compose through a hanging linen awning with foreground shadow, stable verticals, and enough negative space for the interaction to breathe. Sunlight filters from the street and catches only the hands, bag, and a few pieces of fruit. Warm plaster, olive, faded red, kraft paper, and cool street shadow. Natural perspective, f/4-like context, realistic skin and hands, tactile wood and paper, restrained color, no exoticizing costume or staged smile. 4:5 vertical, documentary-editorial realism, no text, brand marks, or souvenir styling.
```

## 7. Eight-frame editorial sequence

Use this request to create a connected set rather than one overloaded prompt:

```text
Create eight distinct production prompts for a quiet spring photo story at a family-run stone guesthouse on a windswept Atlantic island. Keep the same adult traveler, cream-and-charcoal wardrobe, limewashed stone, muted ocean blue, and late-afternoon light across the set. Use these beats: arrival landscape, dark-to-light threshold, stone-and-linen detail, small figure on a terrace, opening a window, local tea ritual with consent, wind moving a curtain, and an empty closing frame after sunset. Vary wide, medium, and close views; use no creator names, no recognizable copied compositions, no logos, and no generic luxury-showroom styling. For each frame, provide the production prompt, negative guidance, 4:5 or 3:2 ratio, locked continuity anchors, and one deliberate variation.
```

## Prompt repair examples

| Weak wording | Repair |
| --- | --- |
| `dreamy luxury hotel, cinematic` | Name the local materials, lived trace, frame relationship, and physical light event. |
| `woman posing beautifully on balcony` | Give her a quiet task, turn her away from camera, and let the balcony and weather remain primary. |
| `vintage film look, lots of grain` | Specify restrained warmth, believable contrast, tactile texture, and optional fine grain only. |
| `perfect symmetrical interior` | Preserve structural order but add a lived asymmetry: towel, book, cup, open window, or moving curtain. |
| `epic desert landscape` | Define scale, silence, surface rhythm, small human trace, and time-specific light. |
