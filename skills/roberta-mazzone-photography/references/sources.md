# Sources, Method, and Limitations

Last researched: **2026-08-09** (Asia/Singapore).

This is an independent analysis of public material. It is not official, affiliated with, endorsed by, or authorized by Roberta Mazzone. Source links identify evidence; they do not license reuse of photographs.

## Method

The analysis combines:

1. Public identity and biography pages that explicitly connect Roberta Mazzone with `_lagiuditta`.
2. A directly attributed interview describing her travel philosophy, composition, camera workflow, aperture preference, and warming choice.
3. A publisher's description and attributed artist statement for the book *Estati d'animo*.
4. Public, credited project pages covering Mediterranean and winter hospitality work.
5. Visual inspection of the public gallery embedded in S1, used only to identify broad recurring traits such as thresholds, foreground framing, natural backlight, negative space, restrained warm color, and small human scale.

Public work was observed for analysis; portfolio images are not bundled with this skill.

## Source register

### S1 — The Lane interview and public gallery

[This Slow Travel Photographer's 3 Favorite Destinations Around The World](https://thelane.com/travel-guide/this-slow-travel-photographers-3-favorite-destinations-around-the-world/)

Supports the strongest workflow claims: architectural training and framing, slow observation, attention to curtains/window light/local interactions, place-led composition, natural light, organic wardrobe and props, lines/textures/negative space, small human details, manual Fuji/RAW capture, f/2.8 or f/4, and a slight warm shift. The page metadata observed on 2026-08-09 listed a modification time of 2025-09-22 but no publication date.

The embedded gallery supplied the sampled visual evidence. It repeatedly showed dark-to-light thresholds, curtains or furniture as foreground layers, stable architecture, warm side/backlight, rich shadows, muted earth-and-sea palettes, fluid neutral clothing, small or turned-away figures, and traces of recent presence.

## Confidence boundaries

### High confidence

- Identity linkage between Roberta Mazzone and `_lagiuditta`
- Architectural education and slow-travel/lifestyle focus
- Natural light and environment-led method
- Use of Fuji, manual mode, RAW, f/2.8 or f/4, and slight warmth
- Sea, architecture, hospitality, daily ritual, and in-between moments as recurring subjects

### Medium confidence

- Threshold framing, foreground veils, stable geometry, small figures, earth-and-sea palettes, and rich shadows as recurring visual tendencies. These come from a bounded public sample rather than a complete catalogue.

### Low confidence or unsupported

- Exact camera body or lens
- Fixed focal lengths
- Named film simulations or presets
- A universal grain level, fade curve, halation recipe, or aspect ratio
- A claim that every photograph is candid or unposed
- Editorial comparisons to specific filmmakers as confirmed personal influences

Treat low-confidence elements as optional creative choices and label them as such.

## Update protocol

Re-check S1, S4, S5, and S9 when the user asks for current work, when this research is more than twelve months old, or when the public body of work has visibly shifted. Record the new access date, separate new observation from earlier evidence, and preserve the unofficial/independent disclaimer.
