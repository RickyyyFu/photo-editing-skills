# Style DNA

Use this reference to translate a named style request into observable, reusable visual decisions. Evidence labels refer to the register in [sources.md](sources.md):

- **Documented**: stated by the photographer or a directly attributed project/interview.
- **Observed**: visible across the sampled public work.
- **Inferred**: a practical interpretation, not a confirmed personal technique.

## North star

Create a sensory record of how a place feels during an unhurried interval. Let architecture organize the frame, natural light reveal time, and a small human gesture make the scene lived rather than staged.

## Composition recipes

Choose one primary recipe per frame and vary the recipe across a series.

1. **Threshold portrait**: dark foreground or room, luminous exterior, subject at the boundary, stable verticals, room for air around the figure.
2. **Architectural still life**: repeated shelves, arches, windows, or floor geometry; one lived object interrupts the order.
3. **Foreground veil**: curtain, linen, railing, furniture, or shade crosses part of the frame and makes the viewer feel present.
4. **Small figure / large place**: figure below one third of frame height, broad negative space, gesture rather than facial expression.
5. **Trace without person**: unmade linen, a towel, cup, open book, shower, or moved chair implies recent life.
6. **Textural landscape**: compress layers of rock, dune, water, vegetation, or facade; prioritize surface rhythm over spectacle.
7. **Quiet action**: person turns, walks, holds pottery, arranges food, opens a window, or pauses mid-task; capture before or after the obvious pose.

## Palette recipes

- **Mediterranean dusk**: chalk white, sand, tobacco shadow, muted sea blue, faded peach sky, one dark silhouette.
- **Sunlit interior**: lime plaster, warm wood, oatmeal linen, terracotta, deep brown shadow, small green or burgundy accent.
- **Desert restraint**: ochre, burnt sienna, bone, camel, dusty olive, near-black tree or frame.
- **Winter hospitality**: warm amber interior, charcoal, cream wool, muted pine, cool blue-white snow outside.
- **Quiet street**: weathered white, limestone, shutter brown, faded green, laundry white, warm gray shadow.

## Priority order

When traits conflict, preserve this order:

1. Place-specific truth
2. Natural light and time
3. Architectural composition
4. Unforced human state
5. Material texture
6. Restrained color
7. Optional grain, flare, haze, or blur

Do not compensate for a weak first five layers with stronger post-production effects.
